Why Sprout?

Designed to be intentional, effective, and completely private.

Intelligent Blocking

Select your most addictive apps. Sprout places a strict overlay on them so you cannot access them out of pure habit.

Earn Your Minutes

No more mindless scrolling. Accomplish goals, wait out a timer, or spend earned "Braincells" strictly to unblock an app for a set duration.

Floating Timer

Stay aware of your time. When you unlock an app, a subtle floating timer reminds you exactly how many guilt-free minutes you have left.
