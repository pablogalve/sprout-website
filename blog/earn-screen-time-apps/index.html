Apps that make you earn your screen time

Updated 14 November 2026

If you want an app that locks Instagram, TikTok or YouTube until you have done something first, such as pushups, a walk, reading or a chore, the category you are looking for is earn-your-time screen time apps. Sprout (the screen time app on iPhone and Android, not the gardening or social media tools with the same name) is built around exactly this: a daily allowance for the apps you choose, a block when it runs out, and minutes back only for activities you complete.

Why earning works when limits do not

A normal limit asks you to stop at the moment you least want to. The block screen offers one more minute, you tap it, and the limit becomes a suggestion. Earning flips the order: the extra time exists, but it sits behind a short real-world task. Ten squats are not hard, and that is the point. They are just hard enough to turn an automatic reach for the phone into a decision.

It also changes what the day feels like. Instead of counting minutes you lost, you see minutes you earned from things you wanted to do anyway: exercise, study, cleaning, a phone-free meal.

How Sprout does it

Pick the apps. Choose the apps that eat your day, usually TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat or games.

Set a daily allowance. You get a set number of free minutes a day. When they run out, those apps are blocked.

Earn minutes back. Pushups and squats are counted with the camera. Steps count from your phone. Timed activities such as studying, deep work, a walk, yoga, cooking, cleaning or meditation pay minutes for the time you spend. You can add your own activities too.

No free extra button. The block lifts because you did something, not because you asked nicely.

Block Reels and Shorts only (Android). On Android, Sprout can block the short-video feed inside Instagram, YouTube and other apps while the rest of the app keeps working.

Your screen time data and app choices stay on your phone. Sprout is free to start on iPhone and Android, with a free trial of Premium.

Other ways to get a similar effect

Screen Time and Digital Wellbeing (free): built-in limits, but nothing to earn and the limit is one tap from being ignored.

one sec and ScreenZen: add a pause before an app opens. Good for reflexive opens, but you can always continue.

Opal: strong scheduled blocking. Time comes back when the schedule ends, not because you did anything.

A fitness tracker plus willpower: works for some people, but nothing connects the steps to the block.

Questions people ask

Is there an app that makes you do pushups to unlock Instagram?

Yes. Sprout blocks the apps you choose once your daily allowance is used up, and one way to earn minutes back is pushups or squats, counted with your phone's camera. It works on iPhone and Android.

Can I earn screen time with chores or homework?

Yes. Sprout has timed activities for studying, cleaning, laundry, cooking and more, and you can create your own. Each one gives minutes back for the time you spend on it.

Does Sprout work on iPhone?

Yes. On iPhone Sprout uses Apple's Screen Time framework to block the apps you pick. On Android it can also block just Reels and Shorts inside apps.

Is Sprout free?

Sprout is free to start. Premium unlocks more blocked apps and features and comes with a free trial.

Start earning your screen time

Pick one app, set a daily allowance, and earn minutes back with pushups, steps, reading or chores. Free to start, on iPhone and Android.
