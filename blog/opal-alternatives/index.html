Opal alternatives: five apps compared

Updated 14 November 2026

Opal is a strong app blocker built around schedules: apps disappear during the hours you choose. People look for an alternative when the price is too high, when their distraction does not follow a schedule, or when they want something that rewards them instead of only restricting them. Here are five options and the honest case for each.

1. Sprout, a daily allowance you earn back

How it blocks: you get a daily allowance of minutes for the apps you choose. When it is used up, they are blocked until you earn more by completing an activity: pushups or squats counted with the camera, steps, studying, a walk, chores or your own activities.

Best for: people who scroll at unpredictable times and keep giving themselves more time. Scrolling is still possible, it just has to be earned. On Android it can block only Reels and Shorts inside apps.

Cost: free to start, Premium with a free trial. iPhone and Android.

2. one sec, a pause before opening

How it blocks: it does not, quite. It shows a breathing exercise before the app opens and asks whether you still want to continue.

Best for: the reflexive opens you never decided to make. It does little against a deliberate scroll.

3. ScreenZen, free friction

How it blocks: a wait before an app opens, open limits and time limits, without a subscription.

Best for: anyone who wants Opal-like control without paying, and is fine with friction instead of a hard block.

4. Brick, a physical switch

How it blocks: a small device you tap with your phone to lock apps. To unlock, you have to tap it again, so leaving it at home makes the block real.

Best for: people who need distance, not software. It costs a one-off hardware price.

5. Screen Time and Digital Wellbeing, free

How it blocks: daily limits and downtime, built into the phone.

Best for: trying first. It fails mainly because "ignore limit" is one tap away when you set the passcode yourself.

How to choose

You need apps gone during set hours: stay with Opal or use Screen Time downtime.

You open apps without thinking: one sec or ScreenZen.

You keep granting yourself more time: Sprout, because extra time has to be earned.

Software never holds you: Brick.

Questions people ask

What is the best free alternative to Opal?

ScreenZen and the built-in Screen Time and Digital Wellbeing limits cost nothing. Sprout is free to start and adds the option to earn time back with activities.

Is there an Opal alternative for Android?

Yes. Sprout, ScreenZen and Digital Wellbeing work on Android. Sprout can also block only Reels and Shorts inside apps on Android.

What is the difference between Opal and Sprout?

Opal blocks apps on a schedule. Sprout gives you a daily allowance and blocks apps when it runs out, and you earn more minutes by doing activities such as pushups, steps, studying or chores.

Try the earn-your-time alternative

Sprout blocks the apps you choose when your daily allowance runs out, and gives minutes back only when you complete an activity. Free to start, on iPhone and Android.
