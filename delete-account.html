Sprout

Delete Account

You can delete your Sprout account at any time. Deleting your account removes your Firebase authentication record (including your email) and stops any further processing connected to that account.

Delete from the Sprout app (recommended)

  1. Open the Sprout app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Follow the confirmation prompt to complete deletion.

App usage data is only stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes that local information. If you need help deleting your account, reach out through the support contact listed in the app store or inside the app.
